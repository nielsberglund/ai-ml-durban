What’s On

⚡ The Mains: Data & AI Community Day Durban

Everything runs off this.

The full day of community talks: 50-minute deep dives, 20-minute lightning talks, and the panel discussion. The themes you’ve been asking for all year, played one more time:

  • AI Agents & Agentic AI: multi-agent systems, orchestration, and implementations that are actually running
  • Generative AI: LLMs, RAG, fine-tuning, and what’s genuinely production-ready
  • Model Context Protocol (MCP): servers, clients, integrations, and use cases
  • AI testing, evaluation & guardrails: how you know it works, and how you stop the slop before it ships
  • AI in CI/CD and responsible AI: shipping safely, and governance that means something
  • Microsoft Fabric, analytics & databases: data engineering, lakehouses, BI, and yes, SQL (still not dead)
  • Streaming & real-time data: Kafka, Event Hubs, Spark, Flink, and data in motion
  • AI for Africa: use cases, challenges, and opportunities on our continent
  • Ways in: using AI to get into tech, getting started as a newcomer, and building something of your own

As always, non-Microsoft tech is welcome, and if your topic doesn’t fit a list, we still want to hear it.

🎸 The Backline: .NET Conf 2026 Community Edition South Africa

The gear the whole band plays through.

A dedicated .NET Conf track, running alongside The Mains all day. Expect:

  • .NET 11: what’s new, what’s changed, and what it means for the code you ship
  • Cloud-native .NET: building, deploying, and running .NET apps in the cloud
  • AI in .NET: building AI-powered applications, agents, and MCP servers with .NET
  • Developer productivity: tooling, IDEs, AI-assisted development, and the workflows that make your day better
  • Architecture & performance: the patterns and lessons that only come from production

Whether you’re a seasoned .NET developer or just starting out, there’s something here for you. And no, you don’t need to write C# to get value from it. AI, and especially MCP, is for everyone, whatever your stack.

The Mains and The Backline run side by side, so you choose what to see. We’ll publish the exact timing as early as possible so you can plan your day.
