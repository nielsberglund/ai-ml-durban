So, What’s with “Plugged In”?

AI Unplugged was about stripping things back to find out what’s real. Plugged In is the next step: now that you know what’s real, get connected to it.

Connected to the tools that change your actual working day, not your slide deck. Connected to an industry that’s hiring, and to the people already inside it. Connected to a community that will still be here in November when you’re stuck at 23:00 on something that should have worked.

Plug into the tools. Plug into the industry. Plug into the community.

That’s the whole idea. And look, we live in a country where staying powered is a skill in itself. We know a thing or two about building anyway. 🔌

You Asked. We Listened. 🎤

Here’s the thing about “for the community, by the community”: it only means something if we actually ask.

So at AI Unplugged II, we asked the room what they wanted to see next. The answers came back fast, and they were remarkably consistent. You wanted developer tools and IDEs. You wanted to know how to test AI and stop the slop before it reaches production. You wanted AI in CI/CD. You wanted more data focus. You wanted hands-on training.

And more than anything else, you wanted a way in. How do I use AI to actually land a tech job? How do I get ahead as a newcomer? We have the highest unemployment rate in the world; how do we help people start something of their own?

We heard you. This entire event is built on those answers. 🇿🇦

Data & AI Community Day Durban: Plugged In

No corporate polish. No death-by-slideshow. No “AI will change everything” hand-waving. Just real people sharing real knowledge with a room full of 200+ developers, data professionals, students, and tech enthusiasts who are hungry to learn.

Plugged In is a FREE, full-day, in-person event for the community, by the community. We keep the friendly Durban energy, the slightly cheeky banter, and the serious technical depth. What’s new this time is the shape of the day: two tracks, and one of them is something we’ve been working on for a while.

Two Tracks. Pick Your Socket. 🔌

⚡ The Mains — our core track

Everything runs off this.

The full day of community talks: 50-minute deep dives, 20-minute lightning talks, and the panel discussion. This is where the community shows up.

Inside The Mains, we’re organising sessions into three strands, drawn directly from what you told us you wanted:

🔌 Earthed — doing it properly

Because anyone can generate. Not everyone can build.

  • AI testing & evaluation: how do you actually know the thing works?
  • Preventing the slop: guardrails, quality gates, and catching the confidently wrong answer
  • AI in CI/CD: pipelines, automation, and shipping AI-assisted code safely
  • Responsible AI: bias, fairness, governance, and the stuff that actually matters
  • Cybersecurity: because every developer and data professional needs to think about this

“Earthed” is the safety connection. It’s the one that stops things from catching fire.

🚪 Ways In: getting into the room, and staying in it

You asked for this louder than anything else, so it gets real weight this time.

  • Getting real with AI: how do you actually use it to land a tech job?
  • Prepping newcomers and the youth: what to learn, in what order, and what to ignore
  • Starting something of your own: entrepreneurship, side projects, and the business case
  • Personal branding, portfolios, and community presence
  • The Future Heroes Student Showcase

🔋 Full Current: the Data & AI spine

The technical depth you already come to us for, plus the more data focus you asked for.

  • AI Agents & Agentic AI: multi-agent systems, orchestration, and real-world implementations
  • Generative AI: LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG, fine-tuning, and what’s actually production-ready
  • Model Context Protocol (MCP): servers, clients, integrations, and use cases
  • Small Language Models: running AI locally, and when you don’t need GPT-scale
  • AI for Africa: unique use cases, challenges, and opportunities on our continent
  • Microsoft Fabric & Analytics: data engineering, OneLake, lakehouses, Direct Lake, and BI
  • Database Development: performance tuning, architecture, indexing, and modern database tech (because SQL is not dead, and we’ll fight anyone who says otherwise)
  • Streaming Data & Real-Time Analytics: Kafka, Event Hubs, Spark, Flink, and data in motion
  • Power Platform & Power Apps: making AI and data accessible to business users
  • .NET & Development: what’s new, what’s next, and practical developer tips

As always, we welcome topics covering non-Microsoft tech, and if your topic doesn’t fit neatly into a strand, we still want it.

🎬 Live Takes — GitHub Copilot Dev Days

Watch it get built. Unedited.

This is the one we’re excited about. For the first time ever, GitHub Copilot Dev Days comes to Durban, and we’re hosting it as a dedicated track.

Dev Days is a global, in-person, community-led initiative running from September 1 to October 31, 2026. The series brings together developers, enthusiasts, and local tech communities to explore the power of GitHub Copilot through practical, hands-on experiences. Open to all developers, each session focuses on real-world workflows and activities centred on GitHub Copilot in the Copilot app and the CLI.

We’re running live, demo-led sessions on real Copilot workflows. Real repos. Real mistakes. Nothing pre-baked, nothing rehearsed into a perfect demo that falls over the moment you try it yourself.

Figure 2: GitHub Copilot Dev Days 2026

About laptops: bring one if you have one; you’ll get more out of the session. But you’ll follow everything either way. We’re not going to pretend every attendee walks in with a Copilot licence, and nobody is going to be left staring at a screen they can’t use. 💻

The Dev Days block runs with The Mains, so you have the opportunity to choose between them. We’ll publish the exact timing as early as possible so you can plan your day.
